How I work

The same method, whatever the industry

  1. 01

    Understand the question

    Start from the decision the business needs to make, then define the KPIs that answer it.

  2. 02

    Prepare the data

    Fix structure and types, standardize values, and document each step so results can be traced.

  3. 03

    Model & analyze

    Build a clean model and choose measures that describe reality: medians, weights, honest denominators.

  4. 04

    Design the dashboard

    Question-led titles, a clear hierarchy and drill-through, shaped by UI/UX training.

  5. 05

    Recommend

    Turn findings into actions, and say plainly what the data cannot show.

About

I study Statistics and Data Science at the Faculty of Commerce (English Section), Benha University. My first two years were in business (administration, HR and marketing), so I approach data from the side of the people who have to make the decision.

Statistics taught me to question an average before trusting it. UI/UX training taught me that a dashboard only helps if people can read it in seconds. I bring both into every project.

I have built dashboards across sales, HR, marketing, education and economics, and I am completing the Data Analysis track of the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI).
